Rings

A ring is made of a few simple parts, but small choices in the shank, the profile and the setting change how it looks and feels on the hand. Use this guide to learn the parts of a ring, compare shank options and profiles, and explore the most popular ring types.

Anatomy of a Ring

Views of a Ring

Shank Options

The shank is the band of the ring that goes around the finger.

Ring Profiles

The profile is the shape of the band when it is cut across, and it decides how the ring feels on the finger.

Ring Types

Here are some popular ring types:

Solitaire

A solitaire ring is a ring with a single gemstone, often a diamond, and may or may not have accent stones. In this ring type, prong setting is the most popular setting type, but the size and number of the prongs can vary to highlight the look and features of the stone. A solitaire ring is mainly used as an engagement ring and given to a woman by her fiancée as a symbol of his commitment to marry her. It is worn on the left ring finger.

Band

A band is a type of ring that possesses the same width all the way round and mainly used for wedding rings.

Eternity Band

An eternity ring, also known as an infinity ring, is a woman’s ring comprising a continuous line of identically cut stones, usually diamonds, to symbolize neverending love.

Half Eternity Band

A half eternity ring is an alternative with the stones across the face of the ring only. The idea behind this type of ring is to reduce the presence of stones all the way round to make it less cumbersome to wear or to reduce the cost.

Fashion

Fashion rings are a type of fashion jewelry that people wear partly for fashion or styling purposes, and partly for functional purposes.

Three Stone

This ring holds three diamonds of the same quality and shape representing the past, present and future of a relationship. Usually the center stone is slightly larger than the other two side stones.

Wedding, Bridal Set

A wedding ring symbolizes marriage as this ring is presented to spouse on a marriage ceremony to exhibit marital commitment. Wedding ring is one of the most popular and demanded jewelry items and it is used in most cultures of the world these days.

Bridal set is a combination of an engagement ring and wedding ring that often match or complement each other.

Heart

A heart ring creates the perfect time and atmosphere for adornments and symbols of love, commitment and strength. This ring is perfect to fit all the love and romance in the air specially on Valentine’s Day.

Navgrah

The word Nav means ‘nine’ and Grah means ‘planets’ in the Sanskrit language. Navgrah ring refers to the nine gemstones related to the nine planets used in Vedic astrology which are listed below with the names of ruling planet associated with each as follows:

  • Ruby - Sun
  • Pearl - Moon
  • Coral - Mars
  • Emerald - Mercury
  • Yellow Sapphire - Brahaspati
  • Diamond - Venus
  • Blue Sapphire - Saturn
  • Hessonite - Rahu
  • Cat’s Eye - Ketu

This ring category is getting more and more famous because of its stylish looks and astrological benefits. Males should wear this ring on their ring finger of their right hand and females should wear it on their ring finger of their left hand.

Journey

A “Journey Ring” is called so because the stones start out small and gradually get bigger in size, which symbolizes the way a relationship grows and changes over the course of time, or over the course of a life’s journey.

Halo

The halo ring is a setting that encircles a centerstone in a collection of round pavé or micro-pavé diamonds. These pavé stones flash with light and focus attention back on the center stone to create interest and draw people’s gaze to the ring and give it a much bigger look.

Reversible

A reversible ring features a reversible center portion with two sides.

Mens

As the name says for itself, these are the rings which are worn by men.
