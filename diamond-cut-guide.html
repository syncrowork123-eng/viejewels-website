Diamond Cut Guide

Learn about different cuts and their impact on the quality of a diamond.

Cut is widely considered the most important of the 4 Cs. It refers not to shape, but to how well a diamond's facets interact with light — determining its brilliance, fire, and scintillation. Even a diamond with excellent color and clarity can look dull if it's poorly cut.

Cut Grade Scale

Grade Description
Excellent Reflects nearly all light entering the diamond, delivering maximum brilliance and fire. Facets are cut with the most precise proportions and angles.
Very Good Reflects most light that enters, producing a high level of sparkle. Slight variances from ideal proportions are difficult to notice with the naked eye.
Good Reflects a majority of light, offering solid brilliance at a more attainable price point compared to higher grades.
Fair Allows noticeably more light to escape from the sides or bottom, resulting in reduced brilliance and a duller overall appearance.
Poor Facets are significantly misaligned or disproportionate, causing most light to escape rather than reflect back through the table.
