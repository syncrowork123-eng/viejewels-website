Nose Pins

A nosepin is a decorative type of jewelry ornament which is worn at the end of the nose through a pierced hole. A nosepin can be seen as a beautiful accessory, a symbol of status, wealth or prestige, or even as an act of rebellion. In Ayurvedic medicine, nose piercing is associated with the female reproductive organs. It is believed that nose piercing helps reduce periodic menstrual pain and discomfort and that it assures less pain during childbirth. Hence, women pierce the left nostril and men pierce the right side.

Nosepin Types

Here are some popular nosepin types:

Solitaire

A solitaire nosepin is a kind of a nosepin comprising of a single or a solitaire stone of any shape and size.

Wired, Hoop

Hoop nosepins are meant to decorate a nose piercing which feature a circular shape that wraps around the nostril.

Screwback

A screwback nosepin is straight like a pipe and fit with a screw. The nosepin is designed to be pushed into the piercing and it needs to be screwed into place from inside the nose, which allows the nosepin to feel more secure, and it’s less likely to fall out.

Fashion

A fashion nosepin is a type of fashion jewelry that people wear partly for fashion or styling purposes and partly for functional purposes.
