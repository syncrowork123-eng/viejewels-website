Earrings

Earrings come in many shapes, lengths and fastenings. Use this guide to compare earring views and back options, and to explore the most popular earring types, from classic studs to long drops and hoops.

Earring Types

Here are some popular earring types:

Stud

Stud earrings are a kind of ear jewelry comprising of a solitaire stone of any shape and size, set in a small component that fits onto the earlobe on straight post and does not dangle down. As there is no visible point of connection they appear to be floating.

Component Options

This stud component on which the diamond is set, is available in the following options:

Basket

Basket-style stud settings have a flat bottom and the stones are set farther from the ear. This stud type is made in a sturdy square or rounded basket setting with four prongs.

Crown

Crown-style settings are made with a flat bottom and the stones sit farther from the ear. The construction of this stud type is more solid, as they are made with more metal and they tend to slightly droop when put on.

Martini

Martini-style settings are shaped like a cone, and their V-shape resembles a martini glass when looked at from the side.

Long

A dangle earring hangs below the earlobe and has movement as it swings to and fro or side to side. Drop, Dangling and Chandelier are various types of long earrings.

Drop

This design mostly comprises of two parts. They consist of an attachment to the earlobe and some ornament that hangs from the attachment, giving a drop look to the piece.

Dangling

A dangle earring is designed in a way that the hanging part sways and dangles below the earlobe and the length of which can reach up to shoulders.

Chandelier

It is a type of decorative earring, with a drop or drops that hangs down from the earlobe and dangles like a chandelier.

Fashion

Fashion earrings are a type of fashion jewelry that people wear partly for fashion or styling purposes, and partly for functional purposes.

Huggie

Earrings that fit closely around the earlobes are referred to as huggie earrings. The thickness of the earring makes them look as though the earrings are “hugging” the earlobe when they are being worn. Most of the huggie earrings are designed with a hinge that allows the wearer to open and close the earrings in order to put them on and take them off.

Hoop

A hoop earring is a circular-shaped earring for pierced ears, which looks like a ring and goes in a loop from the front to back of the earlobe. These earrings are available in many variations and a wide variety of shapes and sizes that include hoops with charms and with other jewelry items to be hung from the hoop.

Journey

A “Journey Earring” is called so because the stones start out small and gradually get bigger in size, which symbolizes the way a relationship grows and changes over the course of time, or over the course of a life’s journey.

Navgrah

The word Nav means ‘nine’ and Grah means ‘planets’ in the Sanskrit language. Navgrah earring refers to the nine gemstones related to the nine planets used in Vedic astrology which are listed below with the names of ruling planet associated with each as follows:

  • Ruby - Sun
  • Pearl - Moon
  • Coral - Mars
  • Emerald - Mercury
  • Yellow Sapphire - Brahaspati
  • Diamond - Venus
  • Blue Sapphire - Saturn
  • Hessonite - Rahu
  • Cat’s Eye - Ketu

This earring category is getting more and more famous because of its stylish looks and astrological benefits.

Reversible

A reversible earring features a reversible center portion with two sides.
