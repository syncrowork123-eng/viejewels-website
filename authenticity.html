Conflict Free Diamonds

Conflict free diamonds are guaranteed to originate from ethical & environmentally responsible sources. These diamonds are free from violence and human rights abuse and are produced with minimal environmental impact.

We, at Vie Jewels, have a zero-tolerance policy toward conflict diamonds. We believe strongly in this cause and have a relationship with everyone we work with to ensure they are part of this ethical process.

Kimberley Process Certification

Kimberley Process Certification Scheme (KPC) is an international certification scheme that was introduced by the United Nations to monitor the trade of diamonds. Every diamond has to meet an extensive list of requirements to ensure it comes from a “conflict-free” source. Once the diamonds have been certified they can be legally traded by members under the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme.

Our every Invoice comes with a written warranty, stating that our diamonds are derived from Kimberley-certified sources and not involved in funding conflict. The warranty statement, which was officially recognized at the creation of the Kimberley Process, reads:

“The diamonds herein invoiced have been purchased from legitimate sources not involved in funding conflict and in compliance with the United Nations Resolutions. The seller hereby guarantees that these diamonds are conflict-free, based on personal knowledge and/or written guarantees provided by the suppliers of these diamonds.”

Through measures such as the Kimberley Process, which tracks diamonds from mine to market, we, in partnership with the United Nations, governments, non-governmental organizations and policies prevent the trade of illegal diamonds.

Jewelry Certification

The authenticity of metal such as gold, platinum, silver as well as stones such as diamonds used in any jewelry piece are reinforced by the certification it comes with which offers confidence and peace of mind to the customers.

At Vie Jewels, we stamp each jewelry piece with metal karat. We also provide a jewelry certificate from independent certification laboratories such as IGI, EGL, etc. who furnish an expert and impartial analysis of a jewelry item that contains all the product details with a photo.

Jewelry Hallmarking

Buying jewelry is an investment and hallmarking is an official mark by an independent authorized Hallmarking Centre certifying the metal fineness or purity.

Our jewelry pieces* are stamped with a hallmark to assure trust and confidence.

*Those made in India
