2D Sketch Designing

From Idea to Drawing

Sketching process is an important part of jewelry modeling and is an effective way for visual and verbal communication. The jewelry design process starts with initial jewelry sketches. The drawings are presented based on the idea, concept, photos provided either by the client or the client can select from the pre-designed collection of the design artist.

Once the design has been conceptualized, the process of designing is carried out by proficient artists using techniques and methods handed over decades. A concept is converted in to actual designs in a process called 2D manual sketch designing which is carried out by hand using black and colored pencils as well as designing softwares.

The jewelry designers will initially compose rough drafts of the sketches in various views or angles. The design is refined and finalized by adjusting proportions, stones, color, etc. The artists will make changes and improvements until the draft is approved by the client.

Sample Draft Sketches

After the draft concept has been approved, the skilled artists then convert the rough draft into final rendered images by further refining the design with the help of pens, pencils, colors and special designing softwares. In this process expected measurements, dimensions, stone sizes, metal weights etc. details are mentioned.
