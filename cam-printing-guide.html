Accurate Wax Prints of Your Design

About Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM)

Our professional technicians will print your CAD design in wax with state of the art machinery which provides the results with accuracy and precision.

Rates

Rates start at as low as $5 per gram for non-castable wax and $10 per gram for castable wax. Rates will be derived based on the complexity of the design.

Procedure

  1. 1

    Step 1

    Send us the STL files to be printed with the required quantity by email at ssdiaminc@gmail.com or by clicking the button below to receive our quotation.

  2. 2

    Step 2

    Confirm our quotation and send us the advance payment as per the instructions mentioned with the quotation.

  3. 3

    Step 3

    Upon receipt of the payment, final wax/resin piece(s) will be printed and mailed to you.
