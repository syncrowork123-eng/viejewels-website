Why Vie Jewels

The Distinguishing Features That Define Vie Jewels

Jewelry on Demand

Jewelry styles showcased on our website are made to order. We stock the master models of each style which are converted into finished pieces on demand.

Customization

A custom design is for the creation of a unique item based on the concept of emotions, ideas or even memorable events that you can recreate in a special singular way.

At Vie Jewels we strive to reflect personal style and taste for each customer to be able to create an exclusive piece of art they have envisioned in alignment with the budget and the timeline provided. Now is your chance to be involved in creating a tailor-made piece that you will cherish, not only for what it represents but also because you oversaw and fine-tuned every detail of its creation through each step of the process.

Savings

By making on-demand jewelry we do not generate any physical inventory but only produce what is ordered. As a result, our Capital is not tied up in Inventories and we save on storage and inventory management costs too. By selling direct to consumers, middleman markup is eliminated.

All these measures help us in reducing the overhead costs tremendously and thereby give us an edge over retail stores and other businesses which enable us to offer quality products at lower prices.

Quality Control

We have set very high-quality standards for our products and services. Strict guidelines have been set and our trained quality control inspectors enforce rigorous quality control at each stage.

Designing

Our skilled artists are well versed in modern trends and simultaneously incorporate traditional values into our jewelry. Our speciality is in custom creating what your heart desires.

Craftsmanship

Our professional craftsmen are skilled and experienced in handcraft as well as use the latest state of the art equipments and resources to craft each masterpiece. For example, our artisans use microscopes for Micropave setting to achieve a rich and elegant finish.

Confidentiality

We highly value your privacy. Your proprietary styles will always stay safe, secure and protected with us as we do not share these with others.

Showcasing Styles

We have thousands of unique ensemble of jewelry designs for you to choose from. We also keep adding to our collection at regular frequencies.

Shopping Experience

At Vie Jewels we offer a near to real vision of each product, including high-resolution images and interactive videos. As a progressive, customer-driven company, we are dedicated to making your diamond and jewelry buying a pleasurable experience.

Concierge Service

Customer satisfaction is our utmost priority. In case you do not find what you’re looking for on our website, get in touch with our helpful experts for a personalized consultation to buy the diamond of your choice or to create the customized jewelry piece of your dream. We listen with sensitivity and provide assistance to fulfill your specific requirement by using our expertise and experience to suit your style, personality and budget.

Customer Service

Customer assistance is an essential part of the before and after-sale activity that we support round the clock. Our professional and courteous customer support team is committed to respond within 24 hours of your query.

No MOQ

We do not impose any restrictions on Minimum Order Quantity requirements. You can order any quantity at ease.

Worldwide Shipping

In our passion to make our products available to everyone around the globe, we ship across most countries worldwide.

Secured Shipment

Each shipment is protected right from our door to your doorstep by full insurance coverage in case of loss while in transit. This security feature is integrated in the shipping charge.

Free Engraving

Every jewelry piece is engraved with its diamond carat weight and metal karatage. Should you need to include a personalized message or a logo, we will be happy to engrave it without any additional charge.

Member’s Lounge

A Vie Jewels exclusive, every customer is assigned a unique password to access the Member’s Lounge. In this section, the customer can privately view all the previously customized jewelry styles ordered by them besides styles showcased for general viewing. Current status of orders, statement of accounts, special offers and many more member’s only exclusive features will be also available to the customers in the Member’s Lounge.

Customized Packaging

You can select from a variety of packaging from paper or wooden boxes with optional features such as LED lights, sound, video LED screens to present with a personalized touch.

Wholesaler Advantage

Special discounted rates are available for volume buyers. We will also reimburse the master model making charges for each jewelry style customized with us when that style is ordered in bulk.
