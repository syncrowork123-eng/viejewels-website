About Us

Four Generations of Diamonds & Fine Jewelry

01

Our Story

Vie Jewels is a fourth generation family business that was established in the early 1900’s. The forefathers started as polished diamond traders, after which each new generation has steered the business to the next level. From trading in polished diamonds to importing rough diamonds and manufacturing them, since over a decade ago, Vie Jewels has developed their own Jewelry manufacturing department. The new generation has initiated the launch of an e-commerce website adapting to the current consumer trends, thereby connecting and catering to consumers globally, by showcasing near to real vision of our products and services available online, in an endeavor to tender a beautiful shopping experience.

03

Our Process

Whether it is Diamonds or Jewelry that you wish to purchase or would like to use any of our services, you can always rely on Vie Jewels to fulfill your needs. You can start by selecting from our extensive Diamond inventory or an ensemble of various made to order Jewelry Styles. Besides these if you have any specific requirements we will custom-make your orders too.

Our Diamonds go through a rigorous process of quality controls and strict assortment standards ensuring consistency and precision.

Our philosophy of creating Jewelry consists of contemporary designs with trendy styles keeping traditional values alive. Right from the designing stage to each step in manufacturing until it is delivered to the consumer, every piece of our jewelry undergoes stringent quality control measures ensuring elegant finishes.

Our Service section demonstrates our commitment to convert your imagination into an actual product with high quality of service, support and advice. Our in-house design team can help you with a variety of professional services which range from creating the perfect bespoke jewelry, to furnishing with high quality rendered images, video animations to superior quality photo enhancing.

A fully integrated diamond and jewelry company with manufacturing facilities in China and India, Vie Jewels provides unbeatable value to our Consumers.

With backgrounds of more than twenty years of hands on experience in manufacturing of Diamonds and Jewelry, earning degrees in Diamond grading and Jewelry manufacturing from renowned Institutions such as GIA, the Directors at Vie Jewels, strongly believe in personal involvement and interaction between the teams and the consumers.

We pride ourselves and recognize the merit and respect the dignity of all team members who work tirelessly and diligently to ensure we achieve this common goal.
