About Image Rendering

Computer-generated image rendering is a powerful selling and advertising tool, allowing customers to visualize and preview their jewelry designs in various angles and views prior to the commencement of the manufacturing stage.

Procedure

  1. 1

    Step 1

    Send us the CAD file(s) with all requirements in detail by email at ssdiaminc@gmail.com or by clicking the button below to receive our quotation.

  2. 2

    Step 2

    Confirm our quotation and send us the advance payment as per the instructions mentioned with the quotation.

  3. 3

    Step 3

    Image(s) in JPEG format will be e-mailed to you for your comments and approval. (We will continue to incorporate all changes until you are fully satisfied.) Final rendered image(s) will be sent to you by e-mail as soon as we receive your final approval.
