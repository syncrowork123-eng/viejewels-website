Bangle Size Guide

Find Your Perfect Fit & Match Your Measurements

How to measureSize finderSize chart

A bangle has no clasp, so it must slide over the widest part of your hand and then rest comfortably at the wrist. The right size depends on the shape of your hand, which is why we match bangles by inner diameter and circumference.

How to Measure

Choose whichever method is most convenient, then match your result in the chart below.

01

Measure Your Hand

  1. Bring your thumb and fingers together, as if slipping on a bangle.
  2. Wrap a measuring tape around the widest part of your hand, across the knuckles.
  3. Note the length in inches and find the closest circumference in the chart.
02

An Existing Bangle

  1. Choose a bangle that slides over your hand comfortably.
  2. Measure its inside diameter edge to edge, in mm or inches.
  3. Find the same inner diameter in the chart.
03

Paper or String

  1. Wrap a strip of paper or string around your hand, as in method 01.
  2. Mark where the ends meet and measure the length against a ruler.
  3. Match that length to the circumference in the chart.

Tips for an accurate size

  • Measure your dominant hand, which is often slightly larger. Measure when your hand is warm.
  • Keep the tape snug but not tight, and do not squeeze the hand while measuring.
  • If you fall between two sizes, choose the larger one so the bangle passes over the hand easily.
  • For a snugger look, choose a size close to your measurement; for a looser, relaxed fit, go up one size.
