Jewelry Certification

Learn How Your Diamonds Are Certified

Every diamond is not just graded — it is certified.

Independent laboratory certification confirms what your diamond is, how it has been graded and how it measures against the world’s quality scales.

Whether you choose a natural or an ethically crafted lab-grown diamond, your piece can be set with stones certified by IGI or EGL.

Our Certification Laboratories

Two respected independent laboratories, each grading to its own standards.

IGI

International Gemological Institute

A globally recognised laboratory that certifies both natural and lab-grown diamonds, widely trusted across the jewelry trade.

EGL

European Gemological Laboratory

A long-established laboratory offering independent diamond grading reports, recognised across European and international markets.

Your certificate laboratory is confirmed with your curated quote.

What Your Certificate Confirms

Each report documents the identity and quality of your diamond.

Diamond Origin

Natural, or ethically crafted Lab-Grown, clearly stated on the report.

Carat Weight

The precise weight of the diamond, recorded to the hundredth of a carat.

Color Grade

D–F Colorless · G–H Near Colorless · I–J Faint Tint.

Clarity Grade

The presence of internal characteristics and surface blemishes, assessed under magnification.

Cut Quality

Proportions, polish and symmetry, which determine how the diamond handles light.

Report Number

A unique number that identifies your diamond and can be checked with the laboratory.
