Ring Size Guide

Find Your Perfect Fit & Convert Between Sizing Systems

How to measureSize finderSize chart

A well-fitting ring should slide over the knuckle with slight resistance and sit comfortably at the base of the finger. Ring sizes vary from country to country, so use the chart below to convert between the most common international systems.

How to Measure

Choose whichever method is most convenient. For best accuracy, we recommend using a ring sizer or visiting a jeweler.

01

Ring Sizer / Gauge

  1. Pick the sizer ring that slides over your knuckle with a little friction.
  2. It should come off comfortably without being loose.
  3. Note the size marked on that ring or on the mandrel.
02

Paper or String

  1. Wrap a thin strip of paper or string around the base of your finger.
  2. Mark where the ends meet and measure the length in mm.
  3. Divide by 3.14 to get the inner diameter, then match it in the chart.
03

An Existing Ring

  1. Choose a ring that fits the intended finger well.
  2. Measure its inside diameter edge to edge, in mm.
  3. Find the closest inner diameter in the chart.

Tips for an accurate size

  • Measure at the end of the day, when fingers are at their largest. Avoid measuring when cold.
  • Measure the same finger the ring will be worn on; your dominant hand is often slightly larger.
  • For wide bands (6 mm and above), consider going up half a size.
  • If you fall between two sizes, choose the larger one.
