Bracelet Types

Here are some popular bracelet types:

Tennis

A tennis bracelet is a flexible bracelet, usually featuring one or multiple rows of stones.

The name came about in 1987 when tennis player Chris Evert lost a diamond bracelet during a match in the US Open. The match was postponed while she searched for her bracelet, a search that was televised around the world sparking a desire for the distinctive diamond bracelets which gained popularity and are now known as ‘tennis bracelets’.

Fashion

Fashion bracelets are a type of fashion jewelry that people wear partly for fashion or styling purposes, and partly for functional purposes.

Men

As the name says for itself, these are the bracelets which are worn by men.
