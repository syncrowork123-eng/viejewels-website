Pendants

The word “pendant” comes from the Old French “pendre” and the Latin “pendere”, both meaning “to hang down.” Use this guide to compare pendant views and bail options, and to explore the most popular pendant types.

Pendant Types

Here are some popular pendant types:

Solitaire

A solitaire pendant is a kind of a pendant comprising of a single or a solitaire stone of any shape and size.

Alphabet

Alphabet pendant collection consists of the pendants with initials, names, words, numerals or a combination of these with a variety of fonts to choose from.

Initial

With a variety of font types to choose from, an initial pendant can be any alphabet from A to Z.

Numeral

Numeral pendants can be any numeral or digits from 1 to 0 or a combination of numbers with a variety of fonts to choose from.

Name

It is a type of pendants that can be a name or a word with a variety of fonts to choose from.

Fashion

Fashion pendants are a type of fashion jewelry that people wear partly for fashion or styling purposes and partly for functional purposes.

Heart

A heart pendant creates the perfect time and atmosphere for adornments and symbols of love, commitment and strength. This pendant type is perfect to fit all the love and romance in the air specially on Valentine’s Day.

Circle

Just as you cannot find the beginning and ending of the circle, a circle pendant symbolizes eternity, which means there is no end and this pendant type can be worn to symbolize eternal love.

Journey

A “Journey Pendant” is called so because the stones start out small and gradually get bigger in size, which symbolizes the way a relationship grows and changes over the course of time, or over the course of a life’s journey.

Navgrah

The word Nav means ‘nine’ and Grah means ‘planets’ in the Sanskrit language. Navgrah pendant refers to the nine gemstones related to the nine planets used in Vedic astrology which are listed below with the names of ruling planet associated with each as follows:

  • Ruby - Sun
  • Pearl - Moon
  • Coral - Mars
  • Emerald - Mercury
  • Yellow Sapphire - Brahaspati
  • Diamond - Venus
  • Blue Sapphire - Saturn
  • Hessonite - Rahu
  • Cat’s Eye - Ketu

This pendant category is getting more and more famous because of its stylish looks and astrological benefits.

Religious

Every faith has jewelry to help inspire those who believe. Cross pendants, om symbol, evil eyes, deities, gods etc. are a few types of religious pendants.

Tanmaniya

One of the most sacred customs in Hindu weddings is tying the mangalsutra which is basically a black and gold beaded chain with a gold or diamond pendant, called Tanmaniya. The word mangalsutra can be deciphered as ‘sacred thread or cord’; as ‘mangal’ means auspicious and ‘sutra’ means thread or cord. It is worn as a symbol of marital dignity and chastity and is a promise between a husband and wife that they will always stay together.

Reversible

A reversible pendant can be worn in two ways by turning the reversible portion around.
