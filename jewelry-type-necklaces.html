Necklace Types

Here are some popular necklace types:

Solitaire

A solitaire necklace is a kind of a necklace comprising of a row or multiple rows of single or a solitaire stone of any shape and size. The stones can be either of a uniform size or they can be graduating in size, with larger stones placed in the center and the smaller stones tapering towards either end.

Fashion

A fashion necklace is a type of fashion jewelry that people wear partly for fashion or styling purposes and partly for functional purposes.

Choker

A choker is a close-fitting necklace worn around the neck. It looks like a collar and does not dangle like a regular necklace.

Navgrah

The word Nav means ‘nine’ and Grah means ‘planets’ in the Sanskrit language. Navgrah necklace refers to the nine gemstones related to the nine planets used in Vedic astrology which are listed below with the names of ruling planet associated with each as follows:

  • Ruby - Sun
  • Pearl - Moon
  • Coral - Mars
  • Emerald - Mercury
  • Yellow Sapphire - Brahaspati
  • Diamond - Venus
  • Blue Sapphire - Saturn
  • Hessonite - Rahu
  • Cat’s Eye - Ketu

This necklace category is getting more and more famous because of its stylish looks and astrological benefits.

Interchangeable

An interchangeable necklace set allows you to become your very own creative designer and change the look of this jewelry kind in various different ways.

Changeable Stone Type

With the ability to change the bigger stones with stones of other colors, the same set can be worn with colors of your choice to match the clothes or your mood.

Interchangeable Type

The interchangeable necklaces allow you to wear the set as a whole, in separate parts or in a combination by interchanging and combining the components of the set.
