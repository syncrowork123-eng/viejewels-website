Jewelry Care Guide

Learn How to Maintain Your Jewelry

Jewelry is one of our most cherished accessories. Here are some tips to take care of your precious possession and enjoy its beauty for years to come.

Store jewelry in separate pouches or compartments of a jewelry box to avoid scratching when not in use.

Do not spray perfume or rub lotion directly or near the jewelry as cosmetics cause tarnishing of metals.

Remove jewelry before participating in physical activity or sports to avoid damaging or scratching.

Remove jewelry when working with harsh chemicals, in hot tubs or when swimming in chlorinated water.

Metal and moisture are enemies. Do not immerse jewelry in water, keep away from water.

Remove jewelry before taking a bath or shower.

Do not clean jewelry with chemicals or tissue papers.

Remove jewelry before going to bed.
