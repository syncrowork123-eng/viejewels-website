Necklace Size Guide

A necklace changes the whole look of an outfit, and its length decides where the piece sits and which neckline it suits. Use this guide to compare standard necklace lengths, then measure your neck to find the fit you like best.

Necklace Lengths at a Glance

Each line shows where a standard length falls on the body.

Necklace Types

Six standard lengths, from closest to the neck to longest:

Collar · 12–13 in (30–33 cm)

Sits snugly at the base of the neck with no drop. Best with open or low necklines, and ideal for statement and wide-band designs.

Choker · 14–16 in (35.5–40.5 cm)

Rests right around the neck, just above the collarbone. A classic with strapless, off-shoulder and boat necklines.

Princess · 17–19 in (43–48 cm)

The most popular length. It falls at the collarbone and suits most necklines, and it is the usual choice for pendants and solitaires.

Matinee · 20–24 in (51–61 cm)

Falls between the collarbone and the bust. A versatile length for work and evening wear with crew and high necklines.

Opera · 28–34 in (71–86 cm)

Reaches at or just below the bust line. It can be worn long or doubled into two shorter strands.

Rope · 36 in and longer (91 cm and longer)

Falls at or below the waist. Wear it long, knotted, or wrapped two or three times for layered looks.

How to Measure

Measure your neck first, then choose a length by how much drop you want.

  1. Wrap a soft tape measure or a piece of string around the base of your neck, where a choker would sit.
  2. Leave one finger of space between the tape and your neck so the necklace is comfortable.
  3. Note the measurement in inches or centimeters. This is your snug length.
  4. Add 2 to 4 inches (5 to 10 cm) for each step down in length you want. A pendant usually looks best at 18 inches.

Already own a necklace you love? Lay it flat, close the clasp, and measure along the chain from end to end.
