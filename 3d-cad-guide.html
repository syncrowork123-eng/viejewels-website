About 3D Computer-Aided Designing (CAD)

Our skilled jewelry CAD designers will convert your idea or a concept to 3D Computer-aided designs, maintaining aesthetic appeal, using state of the art software and provide you with printable CAD files with detailed precision and professionalism.

Rates

Rates start as low as US$10 per CAD file and will be determined based on the complexity of the design.

Procedure

  1. 1

    Step 1

    Send us the images/photos/rough sketches with all requirements in detail by email at ssdiaminc@gmail.com, or click the button below to receive our quotation.

  2. 2

    Step 2

    Confirm our quotation and send us the payment for the options selected.

  3. 3

    Step 3

    Final printable CAD file(s) will be e-mailed to you.

  4. 4

    Step 4

    Image(s) will be e-mailed to you based on your requirements for your comments and approval. We will incorporate all changes until you are fully satisfied.
